About Us

AMAZING SPICE PARK PRIVATE LIMITED is a cardamom etrade operator based in IDUKKI, serving cardamom growers and buyers across the Idukki and Theni districts of Kerala and Tamil Nadu since 2005.

We conduct weekly cardamom auctions at branch locations across the region, connecting local growers with licensed buyers under a transparent auction process. Our operations follow all statutory requirements under GST and the Spices Board of India.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2005

Information we collect. When you register as a grower or buyer with ASPPL, we collect your name, phone number, address, PAN or GSTIN (as applicable), and bank details required for auction settlement. We also record transactional data for each auction you participate in (lot details, sale outcomes, payment amounts).

How we use it. We use this information solely to operate the auction, issue statutory invoices and payment documents, meet GST and Spices Board reporting requirements, and communicate transactional updates via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.

WhatsApp messaging. Registered participants who provide a WhatsApp-enabled phone number receive transactional notifications through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Message content is limited to invoices, payment confirmations, and auction updates. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP or contacting us at amazingspicepark@gmail.com.

Sharing. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with (a) statutory authorities as required by law, (b) our banking partners for payment settlement, and (c) service providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations (including Meta Platforms Inc. for WhatsApp message delivery).

Retention. Transactional records are retained for the period required under the GST Act and Income Tax Act (currently eight years).

Your rights. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by writing to amazingspicepark@gmail.com. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent to amazingspicepark@gmail.com or by post to the registered address above.
